PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated November 18, 2026

We at Comfort.co understand the importance of data privacy and cybersecurity, and we wish to protect our users through complying with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and our own Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy details how and why Comfort.co collects, uses, and processes your information when you engage with our Services, such as visiting or browsing the Comfort.co website, purchasing products from Comfort.co, contacting the staff members through email, or other actions such as leaving reviews and following announcements.

How Personal Data is Collected

What Information Do We Collect?

In order to give you the best service, Comfort.co collects the following Personal Data:

  • First Name
  • Middle Name
  • Last Name
  • Email Address
  • Gender
  • Date of Birth
  • Contact Numbers
  • Billing and Shipping Address

Why Do We Collect Information?

We use the data provided by users for the following purposes:

  • To maintain your account/user profile and grant you access to Comfort.co Services;
  • To process or to complete your purchases and transactions;
  • To ship products to you;
  • To receive and respond to your inquiries and requests for certain products/services;
  • To notify you of any unusual account activity;
  • Improve the layout and/content on Comfort.co according to your preferences;
  • To conduct analyses (such as but not limited to, data analytics and surveys) to analyze how you use our platform so we can improve our Services and products and enhance your customer experience; and
  • To protect you from fraudulent access and transactions on the platform.

Your Privacy Rights While Using Comfort.co Services

As a user and data subject, you have the following rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012:

  1. You have the right to be informed whether personal information pertaining to you shall be, are being, or have been processed.
  2. You have the right to be provided the following information before submitting your personal information. This process will not be conducted without prior notification and consent from you:
    • Description of the personal information to be processed;
    • Purposes for which they are to being collected;
    • Scope and methods utilized of the personal information processing;
    • The identity and contact details of Comfort.co staff;
    • The duration of which the information will be stored;
    • Your right to access, correct, as well as remove your personal information.

If you have any questions or concerns, reading this privacy policy will help you understand your rights to privacy and how your data is handled. If you do not agree with the following policies, please do not use our services. If you still have inquiries, please contact the staff at their respective addresses.
